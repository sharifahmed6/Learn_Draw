## Long Description (Maximum 4000 Characters)
**Learn & Draw: ABC Tracing Kids** is the ultimate handwriting and alphabet practice app designed for toddlers, preschoolers, and kindergarteners. With beautiful animations, fun sounds, and guided tracing, your child will learn to write uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and full words with ease!

Designed by keeping kids' education and safety in mind, the app features a completely offline experience with absolutely no data collection. 

### 🌟 Key Features (Free to Play):
- **Alphabet & Numbers:** Learn to trace Uppercase (A-Z), Lowercase (a-z), and Numbers (0-9) with guided animations.
- **Phonics & Audio:** Fun sound effects and clear voice pronunciations for every letter and word to boost early reading skills.
- **Smart Recommendations:** The app automatically tracks your child's progress and recommends specific letters to practice if they need more help!
- **Daily Reminders:** Set up daily practice habits! The app intelligently sends a friendly offline reminder exactly 24 hours after their last session, ensuring they never miss a day.
- **Completely Offline:** No internet required! Perfect for road trips, flights, or safe screen time anywhere.

### 👑 Premium Features (Unlock Everything!):
Upgrade to our Premium Subscription (Monthly or Yearly) to unlock the ultimate handwriting experience:
- **Cursive Writing:** Unlock complete cursive uppercase and cursive lowercase tracing.
- **30-Day Curriculum:** Access the full 30-day guided learning path for step-by-step handwriting improvement.
- **Custom Words (Type & Trace):** A favorite for parents! Type in your child's own name, favorite animals, or weekly spelling words, and the app will instantly generate tracing animations for them to practice!
- **Free Writing Canvas:** Unlock a blank canvas for kids to draw and write freely using beautiful colors and a magical pencil.

### 🛡️ Safe & Kid-Friendly
- **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All progress is saved locally on your device.
- **Offline Notifications:** Our daily reminders work entirely offline. 
- **COPPA Compliant:** Built specifically for early childhood education with strict privacy standards.
