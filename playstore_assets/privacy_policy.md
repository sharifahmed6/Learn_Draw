# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 8, 2026  
**App Name:** Learn & Draw: ABC Tracing Kids  

This Privacy Policy describes how your and your child's information is collected, used, and handled when you use the **Learn & Draw: ABC Tracing Kids** application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children, and we ensure full compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Designed for Families policies.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
We care about your child's safety and privacy. 
- **No Personal Information:** The App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not track or collect your precise geographic location.
- **No Account Required:** Users are not required to create an account or log in to use the App.

## 2. Local Data Storage & Notifications
All data generated within the App—such as tracing progress, streaks, daily challenges, custom words added by parents, and app settings—is **stored locally** on your device using local storage databases. 
- **No Cloud Servers:** We do not transmit this data to any external servers.
- **Local Notifications:** The App uses local notifications to send daily practice reminders. These reminders are triggered completely offline on your device and do not send any data over the internet.
- If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted from your device.

## 3. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases
The App offers optional premium features through auto-renewing subscriptions (In-App Purchases).
- **Secure Processing:** All subscription payments are processed securely by the respective app stores (Google Play Store or Apple App Store). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card information or any personal financial data.
- **Store Policies:** These transactions are governed by the privacy policies and terms of service of Google Play and Apple.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Advertisements
To support the ongoing development of the App, we may display advertisements in the future. We take your child's safety very seriously:
- **Family-Safe Ads Only:** We will only use advertising networks that are certified by Google Play as compliant with the Designed for Families program (e.g., Google AdMob for Families). 
- **No Behavioral Tracking:** These ad networks will only serve contextual ads. They will NOT track your child's behavior across other apps or websites, and they will NOT use interest-based advertising or remarketing.
- **COPPA Compliant:** Any third-party service provider we work with is strictly prohibited from collecting personally identifiable information (PII) from children.

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
This App is primarily designed for children (toddlers, preschoolers, and kindergarteners). We comply with the requirements of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we do not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or any age), there is no data to share, sell, or expose to third parties.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage parents to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
